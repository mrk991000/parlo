Free to start, private by design

Use free models from Gemini or Groq, or run one on your own PC. Prefer Claude or GPT? Paste your key. Keys are stored encrypted and chats stay on your computer.

First launch tips

Parlo isn't code-signed yet, so your system may ask for a confirmation the first time.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

Click More info, then Run anyway. You only need to do this once.

macOS: "can't be opened because the developer cannot be verified"

Drag Parlo to Applications, then right-click it and choose Open, then Open again. You only need to do this once.

Linux: running the AppImage or the .deb 
chmod +x Parlo-*.AppImage
./Parlo-*.AppImage
sudo apt install ./parlo_*_amd64.deb
What do I need to use it?

Nothing technical. On first launch Parlo walks you through connecting a free model, such as Google Gemini or Groq, in about two minutes. You only need a free account with one of them. You can also run a model on your own computer with Ollama, with no account at all. Already have a Claude or GPT key? You can paste that in instead.
